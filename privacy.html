Privacy Policy for Snip

Snip is a personal video editing tool. It lets the account owner send finished videos to their own TikTok account.

What it uses: when you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos to your account. The token is stored only on the computer where the app runs.

What it does not do: it does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone. Videos are processed on the user's own computer.

You can remove access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites.
